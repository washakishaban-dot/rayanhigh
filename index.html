About the school

A home for learning, rooted in faith

Rayan High School brings young people together in Namanve to study, pray and grow. Our students wear the school's blue and white with pride, and our motto guides everything we do: Blessing is Success.

We believe a good education shapes the whole person: a clear mind, a kind heart and a strong sense of purpose. Come and see the school for yourself.

Our founder

A vision led by Sheikh Busuulwa Ibrahim

Rayan High School began with one conviction: a community grows stronger when its children are given both knowledge and faith. That conviction belongs to our founder, Sheikh Busuulwa Ibrahim.

Sheikh Busuulwa Ibrahim is a visionary who saw what education could change for the families of Namanve and Mukono. He founded Rayan High School to place a good school within reach of the community, and he continues to serve it as both principal and director. In these roles he sets the direction of the school, guides its teachers and keeps its values at the heart of every classroom.

His vision grows from two roots that belong together. The first is empowering the community through education. A child who learns to read well, think clearly and work hard can lift a family, and a school full of such children can lift a whole neighbourhood. The second is faith, and in particular the memorization of the Holy Quran, which he wants to be a living part of school life and not an extra.

Empowering the community

Education opens doors that families could not open alone. Under his leadership, Rayan High School works to give young people from the community the learning, confidence and discipline to build better lives for themselves and for those around them.

Memorizing the Holy Quran

Sheikh Busuulwa Ibrahim wants students to carry the words of Allah in their hearts. He encourages memorization of the Holy Quran alongside their studies, so that faith, good manners and knowledge shape the same young person.

Principal and director

As principal, he leads the work of teaching and learning. As director, he looks after the long-term growth of the school. Together these roles keep the school's mission steady: blessing is success.

Faith in the heart, knowledge in the mind, and a community lifted by both.
